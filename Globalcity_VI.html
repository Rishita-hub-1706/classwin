Global City VI

Location: Rudraram • Permissions: HMDA and RERA • Area: 22 Acres

Explore Master Plan Schedule Visit →

HMDA & RERA Approved

Invest with complete confidence. Global City VI is approved by both HMDA and RERA, ensuring legal transparency, secure ownership, and compliance with government regulations.

Spacious 22-Acre Community

Spread across 22 acres, the project offers a well-planned layout with wide internal roads, open spaces, and a peaceful environment for comfortable living and long-term investment.

Prime Location Rudraram

Located in the rapidly developing area of Rudraram, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to major highways, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and commercial hubs, making it a promising investment destination.

Well-Planned Infrastructure

Designed with modern urban planning, the project features wide roads, proper drainage systems, street lighting, and organized plot layouts that provide convenience and a premium living experience.

High Growth Potential

Shankarpally has emerged as one of Hyderabad's most promising real estate destinations. With rapid development and growing demand, Western County offers strong appreciation potential for investors.

Peaceful & Green Environment

Experience a calm and refreshing lifestyle surrounded by open spaces and greenery. The thoughtfully planned environment provides the perfect balance between modern living and natural serenity.
