Western County

Location: Shankarpally • Permissions: HMDA and RERA • Area: 17 Acres

Explore Master Plan Schedule Visit →

HMDA & RERA Approved

Western County is fully approved by HMDA and RERA, providing a legally secure investment with complete transparency and peace of mind.

Spacious 17-Acre Community

Spread across 17 acres, the project features a thoughtfully planned layout with spacious plots, wide roads, and ample open spaces for a modern lifestyle.

Prime Location Shankarpally

Situated in the fast-growing region of Shankarpally, the project offers excellent connectivity to Hyderabad's IT corridor, educational institutions, healthcare centers, and major transportation routes.

Modern Infrastructure

The layout is designed with quality infrastructure, including wide blacktop roads, street lighting, underground drainage, and a well-organized road network for a comfortable living experience.

High Growth Potential

Shankarpally has emerged as one of Hyderabad's most promising real estate destinations. With rapid development and growing demand, Western County offers strong appreciation potential for investors.

Peaceful & Future-Ready Community

Enjoy a serene environment away from the city's hustle while staying well connected to urban conveniences. The project is ideal for both residential living and long-term investment.
